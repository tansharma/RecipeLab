WHAT'S IN RECIPELAB

Features

What the app does today, what's being finished right now, and what's being considered next. Nothing below the first section is a promise — plans change, and the roadmap carries no dates on purpose.

Shipped

IN THE CURRENT BUILD

The library

  • 47 recipes: recipes inspired by rangefinder bodies and shooting eras, and recipes matched to real film stocks — Tri-X, Portra, Velvia, HP5 and more.
  • Smart Sort — select your camera and the grid reorders: fully compatible first, partial matches in an "Extended Library" section, impossible settings hidden entirely.
  • Search across title, tags and film simulation at once, persisting as you switch tabs.
  • Annotations on every setting — tap a badge to read why that specific value was chosen, with camera-aware wording when a setting had to adapt.

The recipe card

  • A full settings grid — film simulation, dynamic range, white balance, tone, grain, sharpness, colour, noise reduction — with compatibility dimming and fallback hints for your selected camera.
  • A tone-curve visualisation and film-grain descriptor for every recipe.
  • An in-app preview: apply a recipe's tone curve, film colour response and grain to one of your own photos, entirely on-device.
  • A before/after slider, a 6-tile Preview Strip, and a 4-slot Compare view with luminance histograms.
  • Field Reference Mode — a full-screen, high-contrast readout of a recipe's settings for framing shots at the camera.
  • Copy settings, save as an image, print an A6 field card, or share a link.

Making it yours

  • Tweak any recipe into your own saved Variation, with fields disabled where your camera can't support them.
  • Collections, an edit history, and per-camera custom-slot tracking with its own history.
  • Vibe Builder — describe a mood in a sentence and generate a recipe entirely on-device using Apple Intelligence. Burst-generate three at once and compare them.

Getting your data in and out

  • Local export of your variations, in plain or passphrase-encrypted form.
  • Import a variations backup, or a single shared recipe file.
  • Sync through your own iCloud account — off by default, on with one explicit toggle. The bundled library never syncs; only what you create does.

Camera and system integration

  • In-app camera capture and full-resolution export back to Photos, entirely on-device.
  • Spotlight search, Siri Shortcuts, and Home Screen / Lock Screen widgets for a recipe of the day and your last-used recipe.
  • A Mac and iPad shell with a proper sidebar, alongside the iPhone layout.

In flight

BEING WORKED ON NOW

Testing and polish before a wider release

A full audit of the test suite against every feature's stated coverage, and a CI pass so the guardrails this app already runs locally — the test suite and the design-token check — run automatically, not just by hand.

On the roadmap

CONSIDERED, NOT COMMITTED — NO DATES

An optional cloud fallback for Vibe Builder

On-device generation is, and will stay, the default — it needs no account, no key, and never leaves your device. A cloud option is being considered strictly as a fallback for hardware that can't run the on-device model, using your own API key, never automatic and never a silent substitute for the on-device path.

This is currently paused rather than in progress, partly to see whether an upcoming on-device model update closes the gap it would exist to cover.
